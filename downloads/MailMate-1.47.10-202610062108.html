MailMate 1.47.10

Fixes a crash when clicking Add Event on an email that mentions a video call or includes an address. Stops Mail from hanging for a minute or two when MailMate checks in the background which emails you already answered: with Full Disk Access, MailMate now reads your sent mail straight from disk in milliseconds instead of asking Mail, and the slow fallback is time-limited and backs off for 6 hours. The background sent-mail scan and the learn-from-your-edits pass read from disk too, so the MailGuard brake no longer trips every ten minutes.
